Waypoint Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Waypoint is a trip planning app built by Marcus, Grace, Joshua and Dylan as a student project at The IIE Emeris School of Computer Science. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Contact: ST10438409@myemeris.edu.za

What we collect

Account details. When you sign in with email or Google we receive your email address, display name and profile photo link. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.

Trip content you create. Trips, itinerary days, flights, lodging, car hire, saved places and notifications. This is stored on your device and synced to our server so it is available after a reinstall or on another device.

Device notification token. A Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send trip reminders to your device.

Location. If you allow location access, your approximate or precise location is used to show nearby places in Explore. It is used while you use the app and is not stored on our server.

Anonymous usage counters. The app increments anonymous counters (for example how many lookups were made) to stay within free API limits. These counters contain no personal information.

Third party services

To provide features, the app sends only what is needed to these services:

  • AirLabs: flight numbers you look up
  • LocationIQ: place searches and your coordinates for nearby places
  • OpenWeatherMap: destination coordinates for weather
  • ExchangeRate-API: currency codes for conversion
  • Wikipedia: destination names for summaries
  • CounterAPI: anonymous usage counters
  • Firebase (Google): sign-in and notifications

Each service has its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers
  • No analytics or crash tracking libraries
  • We do not sell or share your data for marketing

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are handled by Firebase and never stored by us. API keys are not stored in the app source code.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data: use delete account in Profile. This removes your account and the trip data stored on our server.
  • Location: you can deny or revoke location permission in system settings. Explore still works with a searched city.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in system settings.
  • Backup: Android may back up app data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on. You control this in your device settings.

Children

Waypoint is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
